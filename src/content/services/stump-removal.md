A stump is the one part of a tree removal you can postpone, which is why so many Conroe yards have one wearing a planter pot like a hat. The problem is that postponing is not neutral here. Warm, wet, and bug-rich is the exact recipe for a stump to become a project, and Montgomery County serves all three.

## What actually happens to an ignored stump?

It gets colonized. Termites and carpenter ants treat softening pine stumps as starter homes, and neither species respects the property line between the stump and your slab. Fungus moves in with every wet spell. Meanwhile sweetgum, hackberry, and water oak stumps refuse to accept the situation and throw up sucker shoots all growing season, which is how a removed tree becomes a permanent shrub-fighting subscription. The [Texas A&M AgriLife Extension](https://agrilifeextension.tamu.edu/) covers the insect side in cheerful detail if you want the entomology.

## How does removal work and how deep does it go?

A carbide-toothed grinding wheel chews the stump into mulch, working down several inches below grade for a standard finish, deeper on request. Standard depth disappears the stump for lawn purposes: soil and sod go over it and the yard reads as if the tree was never there. Building, paving, or replanting on the spot calls for a deeper grind plus attention to the [lateral roots](/services/stump-grinding/root-removal/), which is a different scope worth naming up front. Either way the grindings are yours to keep as mulch or ours to haul with the rest of the [debris](/services/land-clearing/debris-removal/).

## What does it cost?

Diameter, depth, access, and count set the price, and multiples on one visit are cheaper per stump than one-offs, which matters after a [clearing job](/services/land-clearing/lot-clearing/) leaves a lot with a dozen of them. Utilities get located before grinding near any place lines could run; irrigation heads and shallow pipe are the usual Conroe surprises, so flag them when you walk the crew through. The estimate is free, in writing, and honest about which finish your plans for the spot actually require, whether the stump came from our [removal](/services/tree-removal/) or somebody else's chainsaw weekend.
