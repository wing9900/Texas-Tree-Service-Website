Montgomery County does not get grazed by storm seasons; it gets invoiced by them. The May 2024 derecho and Hurricane Beryl arrived eight weeks apart, and between them they turned thousands of unprepared trees into roof claims and week-long [Entergy outages](https://www.entergytexas.com/). The homeowners who fared best were mostly the ones whose trees had been thinned, cleaned, and cleared off the house while the sky was still blue.

## What does storm prep trimming actually do?

It removes the ammunition and lowers the sail. Deadwood is the ammunition: every dead limb in a crown becomes a projectile at 60 miles per hour, and the pines here stockpile them. The sail is the crown itself, and selective thinning lets gusts bleed through instead of loading the trunk and roots like a lever. Add clearance work that lifts limbs away from shingles, gutters, and siding, and a storm has to work much harder to hand you a claim number.

## Which Conroe trees are the repeat offenders?

Loblolly pines top the list, not because the species is weak but because of where they stand: 90 feet tall, shallow-rooted in sandy soil, and planted decades before the houses that now sit under them. Water oaks are the quiet second, prone to shedding huge limbs and to hiding trunk decay behind a full crown. Sweetgums split at their forks. An experienced eye can rank the trees on your lot in one walk, which is exactly how the estimate works, and it is free.

## Is it too late once a storm is named?

Mostly, yes. When a system enters the Gulf, every crew for a hundred miles is triaging, and pre-storm panic trimming done fast tends to be done badly. Get the work finished in the off season, then spend storm week checking the [NWS Houston forecast](https://www.weather.gov/hgx/) instead of the sky over your pine. If the season beats you to it anyway, the [emergency line](/services/tree-removal/emergency-tree-removal/) is the answer, and afterward the [cleanup crew](/services/land-clearing/debris-removal/) puts the yard back. For trees a trim cannot make safe, the honest path is [hazardous removal](/services/tree-removal/hazardous-tree-removal/) while it is still a choice and not an event, and for keepers with weak forks, [cabling](/services/tree-health/cabling-bracing/) adds mechanical backup before the wind tests them.
