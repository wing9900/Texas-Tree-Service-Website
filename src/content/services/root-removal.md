Roots are the part of the tree Conroe homeowners meet last, usually through the mower deck. The trunk is long gone, the stump was ground sometime in a previous administration, and the yard still has hard gray ridges surfacing through the St. Augustine like whales.

## Why do roots surface in Conroe lawns?

Shallow soil habits. In the sandy loam through this part of Montgomery County, big trees run their structural roots wide and close to the surface, and decades of rain and mowing slowly expose the tops. Water oaks and sweetgums are the classic offenders, and pines leave a radiating spoke pattern that outlives them by a decade. Once exposed, roots get hit by mowers, trip feet, and shrug off attempts to bury them, since a living root thickens and a dead one just waits.

## What does removal involve?

For dead root systems, it is grinding and extraction: the same machine that handles [stumps](/services/stump-grinding/stump-removal/) walks the visible runners, and stubborn sections come out by trencher or by hand. For roots from a living tree you intend to keep, it is surgery with a budget: cuts are kept clean, minimal, and away from the trunk, because roots are the tree's anchor and pantry at once. Guidance from the [ISA](https://www.treesaregood.org/treeowner) puts real limits on how much a tree can lose safely, and around here post oaks deserve special caution, since root damage is the number one reason mature ones die after nearby construction.

## When is root removal part of a bigger job?

Most of the time. Beds, walkways, and patio projects want the ground cleared before anything is laid. Foundation and driveway conflicts pair cutting with a [root barrier](/services/tree-health/root-barrier-installation/) so the fix holds. And a lot being prepped to build runs roots, stumps, and brush as one [clearing scope](/services/land-clearing/lot-clearing/). Whichever version yours is, the estimate walks the actual yard and puts the whole answer in writing, including what happens to a keeper tree whose roots are in the blast radius, before any digging starts.
