Brush is mostly air. That is the secret the chipper exploits: a limb pile the size of a garage compresses into a knee-high mound of chips, because the machine removes the air and the awkwardness in one pass. It is why chipping rides along on nearly every job we run in Conroe, and why the standalone service exists for everyone fighting a pile of their own.

## Why chip instead of haul or burn?

Volume, rules, and use. Hauling raw brush pays by the trip, and raw brush wastes a trailer; chips cut the trips to one. Burning collides with Montgomery County reality: [burn bans](https://tfsweb.tamu.edu/) eat much of the summer, neighbors in subdivisions have opinions, and pine slash burns like it has somewhere to be. Chips, meanwhile, are a product. Spread under trees they do what forest floor does, holding moisture and feeding soil as they rot, which is the cheapest [tree health](/services/tree-health/deep-root-fertilization/) intervention there is. Half our customers keep every chip the job produces.

## What feeds the chipper on a typical Conroe job?

Everything the county throws down. Storm limbs after each front, the endless shed material of mature loblollies, trimming brush from a [canopy job](/services/tree-trimming/canopy-trimming/), and the serious tonnage that [lot clearing](/services/land-clearing/lot-clearing/) generates, where the chipper is the difference between a clearing budget and a hauling budget. The one thing that gets sorted out rather than chipped through is suspect wood: material from [beetle-killed pines](/services/tree-health/insect-borer-treatment/) gets handled so the chips do not spread the problem, and wilt-suspect oak is treated with the same caution.

## What does the service look like on the ground?

The chipper positions at the pile or the curb, the crew feeds it in a steady rhythm, and the chips blow into the truck or onto the tarp-lined spot you choose. An hour of machine time erases what a homeowner spends a month of weekends dragging. Pair it with [debris removal](/services/land-clearing/debris-removal/) when some of the pile is log wood, or book it standalone for the brush mountain that came with the house. Like everything here, the price is written before the machine starts, based on the actual pile rather than a phone guess.
