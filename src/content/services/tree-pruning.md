A tree never stops editing itself. Branches shade each other out and die, forks grow into each other, storm wounds sprout desperate clusters of weak shoots. Pruning is just doing that editing on purpose, with a plan, before Conroe's weather does it at random over your driveway.

## What does proper pruning actually remove?

Deadwood first, always: the gray, bark-shedding branches that loblollies and water oaks hold for years, each one a projectile waiting on a windy day. Then problems in the making, like crossing limbs sawing at each other's bark, tight V-shaped forks collecting rot, and water sprouts crowding the interior. What a proper prune never does is strip the inner growth or top the leaders. The [ANSI A300 standard](https://www.treesaregood.org/treeowner/pruningyourtrees) exists precisely because those shortcuts wreck trees, and it is the rulebook every cut here follows.

## Why does timing matter more for oaks?

Because of oak wilt. The disease rides sap-feeding beetles to fresh wounds, and Texas foresters ask everyone to avoid pruning oaks from roughly February through June when the risk peaks. Montgomery County is not the epicenter that the Hill Country is, but the disease is present in Texas oaks, the precaution costs nothing, and cuts on oaks get sealed here regardless of the calendar. Pines and most other Conroe species can be pruned essentially year round.

## Is pruning worth it on a tree that looks fine?

That is exactly when it is worth the most. A structural prune on a young red oak in Grand Central Park costs a fraction of what the unpruned version of that tree will cost its owner in 2045. Mature trees on older streets, like the established hardwoods around downtown Conroe, mostly need maintenance: deadwood out every few years and problems caught early. If an inspection turns up something bigger, decay, dieback, or beetle activity, you will hear about it straight, with [health treatment](/services/tree-health/tree-disease-treatment/) or [cabling](/services/tree-health/cabling-bracing/) when the tree can be kept and a pointer to [removal](/services/tree-removal/hazardous-tree-removal/) only when it cannot. Before storm season, pair pruning with a [wind-focused trim](/services/tree-trimming/storm-preparation-trimming/) and your trees go into summer with their paperwork in order.
