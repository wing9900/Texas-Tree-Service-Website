Clear Lake grew up with the space program, and so did its trees. The neighborhoods built around NASA's [Johnson Space Center](https://www.nasa.gov/johnson/) in the 1960s and 70s were planted with loblolly pines and oaks that are now sixty feet tall and standing over one-story roofs from Clear Lake City to Bay Area Blvd. Clear Lake Forest and El Dorado are full of them. We run crews down from Pasadena in about 15 minutes, with an ISA Certified Arborist reading every tree first.

## What is killing the tall pines in Clear Lake?

Most often pine bark beetles moving into trees already stressed by drought or root damage. By the time a Clear Lake pine browns from the top, the beetles have usually finished it, and a dead loblolly gets brittle fast. The 2,500 acres of preserve at Armand Bayou Nature Center show what these pines and hardwoods do with deep, undisturbed soil, and it is worth knowing how far a yard tree on compacted fill is from that. Catching the neighbors' outbreak early is the whole game: [insect and borer treatment](/services/tree-health/insect-borer-treatment/) can protect the pines still worth saving, and [tree disease treatment](/services/tree-health/tree-disease-treatment/) sorts out what is actually wrong before anything is cut.

## How does a 60-foot pine come down between Clear Lake houses?

In sections, rigged from the top, with the arborist calling the order of cuts. The patio homes and cul-de-sacs around Sylvan Rodriguez Park in Clear Lake City proper leave no room to fell anything whole, so [large tree removal](/services/tree-removal/large-tree-removal/) here is climbing work: piece by piece, lowered on ropes, with the lawn intact when the trailer leaves. Stump grinding is included, not extra.

## Can a healthy but oversized tree be made storm-safer without removing it?

Often, yes. [Crown reduction](/services/tree-trimming/crown-reduction/) shortens the canopy to sound lateral limbs so hurricane wind has less to grab, which matters this close to Galveston Bay. It is proper ANSI A300 work, not topping, and the tree keeps its health and its shape.

## Whose tree rules apply in the Clear Lake area?

Whichever city your lot actually sits in, and that is rarely obvious from the mailing address. Clear Lake is a region rather than a municipality, and the boundaries run through it in ways that catch people out: most of Clear Lake City is Houston, the Armand Bayou side is Pasadena, a small eastern slice belongs to Taylor Lake Village, and Webster, Seabrook, El Lago, and Nassau Bay each write their own rules besides. Two houses a few streets apart can sit under two different codes.

That distinction changes the answer. Houston, for one, leaves existing single-family yards out of its Chapter 33 requirements but still controls the right-of-way strip between your sidewalk and the street. Deed restrictions are a separate layer that state law does not touch, and around here the HOA is often the stricter authority of the two. We work out which set applies to your address at the free estimate instead of guessing from a zip code, whether you are near Bay Area Park or further south toward Baybrook.
