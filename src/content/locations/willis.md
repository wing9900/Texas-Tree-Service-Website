## How far is Willis from Conroe?

About fifteen minutes north on I-45, which makes Willis one of the closest areas we cover. For emergency work that matters more than anything else on this page. A tree on a roof in Willis at two in the morning gets a crew faster than almost any other address we serve.

We work the 77318 and 77378 ZIP codes, which spans the Lake Conroe north shore, the older parts of town along FM 1097, and the growing corridor toward FM 830 and FM 2432.

## Which parts of Willis do you cover?

All of it, and the mix is unusually wide for one town. Seven Coves sits on a peninsula in Lake Conroe surrounded by the Sam Houston National Forest, and waterfront removals there need the same rigging approach as the west shore. The Woodlands Hills, built north of town by the same developer as The Woodlands, brings newer homes on lots where builders left mature pines standing close to the foundation.

Older Willis neighborhoods east of I-45 are different again: larger lots, less HOA oversight, and trees that have not been touched in thirty years. Panorama Village sits just south of Willis on its golf course, and those fairway lots carry some of the tallest pines in the area.

## Why does Willis get so much storm damage?

Because of what sits next to it. Willis is wedged between open water on one side and the Sam Houston National Forest on the other. Wind coming across twenty two thousand acres of Lake Conroe arrives at north shore properties with nothing to slow it, and homes backing onto the forest have a treeline of tall, untended pine and hardwood leaning toward them.

The 2024 storm season made the point. The May derecho and Hurricane Beryl both moved through this corridor and left the same pattern: snapped pine tops, uprooted water oaks in saturated soil, and driveways blocked by limbs that fell across power drops.

Two services carry most of this. [Emergency tree removal](/services/tree-removal/emergency-tree-removal/) when the tree is already down or hanging, and [storm preparation trimming](/services/tree-trimming/storm-preparation-trimming/) before the season, which reduces sail area on the trees closest to the house. The National Weather Service Houston and Galveston office publishes [local storm outlooks](https://www.weather.gov/hgx/) that are worth watching from June onward.

## Do you clear land and acreage in Willis?

Yes, and it is a large share of what we do here. Willis is growing fast off Lake Conroe proximity, and new sections keep opening north and west of town. Raw acreage in this area carries heavy yaupon understory, tangled brush, and standing dead pine that has to come out before anything can be built or fenced.

That work splits into [lot clearing](/services/land-clearing/lot-clearing/) for building sites and [brush hogging](/services/land-clearing/brush-hogging/) for pasture and fence lines. On finished jobs, [debris removal](/services/land-clearing/debris-removal/) is what separates a cleared lot from a lot with a burn pile sitting on it for six months.

## Do you take the wood away?

Yes. Brush is chipped on site and hauled out, and trunk wood is cut down and removed. If you want logs left for firewood, say so before the crew starts and they will be cut to length and stacked where you want them.

Nothing gets left behind and nothing gets buried on the property. That includes the stump, which we grind below grade so the area can be filled and reseeded.
