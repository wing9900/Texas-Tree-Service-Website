South Houston is a small city with big trees. Its blocks were platted a century ago, so the lots are narrow, the houses are close, and the pecans, hackberries, and oaks that grew up between them now touch rooflines and service drops on both sides of the fence. We are the shop next door on Crenshaw Rd in Pasadena, the same road the YES Prep Southeast campuses sit on, which puts most South Houston driveways less than ten minutes from our gate.

## How do you remove a tree from a tight South Houston lot?

In pieces, from the top down. The century-old blocks around South Houston High School on Shaver St have no gate wide enough for machinery, so the climber rigs each section, lowers it on ropes, and the wood goes out through the side yard by hand. It is slower than dropping a tree in an open field, and it is the only safe way here. When a storm has already put the tree on the house, the same crew runs it as an [emergency tree removal](/services/tree-removal/emergency-tree-removal/) with the phone answered around the clock Monday through Saturday.

## What about the stump in a yard this size?

On a small South Houston lot, the stump is the yard, so grinding is included with every removal we do. On the streets off Theta St near AB Freeman Elementary, and around Pearl Hall Elementary, one old pecan stump can take up a quarter of the usable back yard. If you inherited stumps from someone else's work, [stump removal](/services/stump-grinding/stump-removal/) and [root removal](/services/stump-grinding/root-removal/) reclaim the ground for grass, a shed, or a garden bed.

## Can overgrown trees near the lines be trimmed safely?

Yes, with the clearances the utility expects. South Houston's overhead drops run close to a lot of canopies, and the same is true across the Edgebrook blocks on the Houston side of the city line. Our [canopy trimming](/services/tree-trimming/canopy-trimming/) keeps limbs off your roof and away from your service line, using reduction cuts back to sound laterals. The International Society of Arboriculture covers why proper pruning beats topping every time at [treesaregood.org](https://www.treesaregood.org/).

## Do you need a permit to remove a tree in South Houston?

For a tree in your own yard, usually not, and we confirm it for your specific address before anything is scheduled. Permit rules are set city by city in Texas, so the answer depends on which side of a line your lot sits on, and we would rather check than guess.

State law does give you one protection everywhere. Under Texas Local Government Code Sec. 212.905, a city cannot charge you a tree mitigation fee for removing a tree under 10 inches in diameter, measured 4.5 feet off the ground, at your own one- or two-family home. Easement and right-of-way trees are the exception in every city, and anything touching the conductors themselves belongs to [CenterPoint Energy](https://www.centerpointenergy.com/en-us/services/electricity/vegetation-management) vegetation management, not to us. None of this is legal advice, and ordinances do get amended.
