Tree Removal

Conroe grows the kind of trees that make removal a serious job: loblolly pines pushing past 90 feet, water oaks going hollow from the inside, sweetgums split by the last windstorm. Every removal here comes down in controlled sections, roped and lowered piece by piece, so nothing free-falls near your roof, fence, or driveway. You get a written price before the first cut, and when the crew leaves, the wood is hauled and the yard is raked. See all tree removal services.

Includes: Hazardous Tree Removal · Emergency Tree Removal · Large Tree Removal

Tree Trimming

Good trimming is preventive medicine for Conroe trees. Thinned canopies catch less wind in a storm, limbs come off the roofline before they scrape a hole in it, and dead wood gets removed before it picks a landing spot on its own. Every trim follows the ANSI A300 standard, which governs where cuts belong and how much live growth a healthy trim takes, so your trees come out of it stronger instead of scalped. See all tree trimming services.

Includes: Tree Pruning · Canopy Trimming · Storm Preparation Trimming · Crown Reduction

Stump Grinding

A removal is not finished while the stump is still sitting there. In Conroe yards a leftover stump feeds termites and carpenter ants, sprouts suckers all summer, and blocks every future plan for that corner of the lawn, from sod to fence posts. Grinding takes the stump well below grade, the surface roots with it if you want them gone, and leaves you with soil you can actually plant instead of a slowly rotting obstacle. See all stump grinding services.

Includes: Stump Removal · Root Removal

Land Clearing

Raw land around Conroe does not stay raw politely. Yaupon and dewberry close over a neglected acre in a couple of seasons, volunteer pines come up like weeds, and the lot you bought to build on becomes the lot you cannot walk across. Clearing work spans the whole range: brush hogging that knocks pasture back to usable, selective clearing that keeps your best hardwoods standing, and full site preparation with everything hauled off. See all land clearing services.

Includes: Lot Clearing · Brush Hogging · Debris Removal · Wood Chipping · Commercial Tree Services

Tree Health & Preservation

The cheapest tree work is the kind that keeps a good tree standing. Conroe's pines run a constant gauntlet of bark beetles, its oaks decline quietly for years before anyone notices, and its fast-growing shade trees split at weak forks in the first serious blow. Health work catches those problems while they are still fixable: honest diagnosis first, then treatment, cabling, or a straight answer that the tree is past saving. See all tree health & preservation services.

Includes: Deep Root Fertilization · Tree Disease Treatment · Insect & Borer Treatment · Tree Cabling & Bracing · Root Barrier Installation
